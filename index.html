01 What it does

Three jobs, one question bank.

Set a paper, let students practise, play the chapter with the class — all drawing on the same tagged set of questions.

FOR TEACHERS

Question papers

Pick chapters and a mark scheme, auto-fill, adjust by hand. Export to PDF for the printer and Excel for the records.

FOR STUDENTS

Practice & tests

Three difficulty levels per chapter, unlimited attempts, and a score with a chapter breakdown the moment they submit.

FOR THE CLASSROOM

Live games

Classroom Quiz on the projector, KBC-style Live Quiz, and crosswords built from chapter vocabulary. A game period is still syllabus time.

02 Where the questions come from

6,000+ questions. Every one checked by a human.

Every question in the bank is checked by a person before it reaches a student — the wording, the answer and the chapter tag. Your department's own papers go in alongside them, and your staff can edit any question in any chapter.

  • Human-verifiedA person checks every question, answer and tag before it goes into the bank.
  • Your own papers, keptWord, PDF and scans of the papers your department already sets become part of the same bank.
  • Yours to correctAny teacher on your staff can edit, retag or remove a question. Nothing is locked.

05 FAQ

The questions principals ask.

How are the questions checked?

Every one of the 6,000+ questions in the bank is verified by a human before it is added — the question, the answer and the chapter tag. Your department's own papers go in alongside them, and your staff can edit or remove any question in any chapter.

Is our students' data safe?

The school owns its data. We store only what runs classes, never sell it, and show no ads. Hosting region, retention and data-processing terms are confirmed in writing before you sign.

Is the content organised by syllabus?

Organised chapter-wise by the syllabus for classes 8 to 10. Your staff can add or edit questions in any chapter.

What devices do we need?

A browser, on what you already have. Classroom Quiz needs one projector or TV plus the teacher's device; students can practise on a lab computer or their own phone.

How long does setup take?

We do it: classes, sections, subjects, staff accounts. Typical onboarding is about [X working days] — a placeholder until we commit to a date in your quote.

What if we stop using Zero?

You take it with you. Question banks, papers and results export to PDF and Excel at any time, including on the way out.
