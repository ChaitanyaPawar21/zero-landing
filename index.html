FAQ

The questions principals actually ask.

Is our students' data safe, and where is it stored?

The school owns its data. We store only what is needed to run classes — a student's name, class and login, plus their practice and test records. We do not sell data and there is no advertising on the platform.

Hosting region, retention period and our data-processing terms are confirmed to you in writing before you sign, so your trustees can read them on paper rather than take our word for it.

Is the content aligned to CBSE?

Lessons, question banks, practice sets and tests are organised chapter-wise by the CBSE syllabus for classes 8 to 12, in the order your teachers teach. Your own staff can add or edit questions in any chapter, so anything specific to how your school teaches a topic goes straight in.

How long does setup take?

We do the setup: classes, sections, subjects and staff accounts. Your side of it is naming a coordinator and handing over the class list.

Typical onboarding runs about [X working days] — a placeholder until we commit to a date in your quote. Teachers can start authoring questions on day one; they do not wait for us.

What devices do we need?

A browser, on whatever you already have — school computers, the teacher's laptop, or phones. Nothing to install from an app store.

Classroom Quiz needs one projector or TV and the teacher's device, since the class plays off the one screen. For practice and online tests, students can use a computer lab, shared devices, or their own phones at home.

Does it work on low bandwidth?

Lessons, flashcards, question banks, practice and written tests are text-and-diagram based, so they are light on a slow line. Video lectures are the one part that genuinely needs bandwidth — on a weak connection they are better watched at home.

We test the live games on your school's actual connection during the demo, not on ours.

Can our teachers use their own question papers?

Yes, and most do. Import existing questions from Word files, PDFs or scanned images — the scans go through OCR — and they become part of your school's own question bank. From there the same questions feed written papers, online tests and the classroom games.

What training and support do we get?

Onboarding includes training for the staff who will do the work: the teachers authoring questions, the ones running classroom games, and the office staff managing students and sign-ups.

Training format and support hours are set out in your quote.

What happens to our data if we stop using Zero?

You take it with you. Question banks, papers and results export to PDF and Excel at any time, including on the way out, so a year of your teachers' work stays yours. Deletion of student records after you leave is covered by the same written terms as hosting and retention.
